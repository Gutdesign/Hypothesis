## What worked in the frame stage

- Forcing "who exactly" (01_sharpened) surfaced that the calc scope was fuzzy from the start — going from "интерактивные расчёты" to one named calc (ВТ) to founder-driven expansion to 4 named calcs (ВТ, эвакуация, условные блюда, водоподготовка) happened only because the template demanded specificity before moving on.
- 02_assumptions' "riskiest assumption" question worked as intended — founder immediately named positioning dilution (S1) himself, unprompted, when asked to review the draft. That assumption then correctly turned out to be one of the two live disputes in the engine-zone symmetry check.
- 03_priors caught something real: founder predicted "no ready calculators exist" for 3 of 4 topics and that turned out wrong for 2 of them (condition calc + pool water treatment already have competitors). Recording the prior before research let this divergence be visible instead of silently absorbed as hindsight.
- Re-reading 00_intake after founder rewrote it (adding personal ВТ project context, the 20-30% adoption estimate, "just launched, low traffic" framing) forced a full pass back through 01/02 — this is exactly the kind of cascading update the frame zone is supposed to support, and it worked without needing to restart from scratch.

## What felt weak or like a ritual

- The Discovery-type section in 04_devils_advocate was pure dead weight for this product-type hypothesis — just a one-line "N/A" was correct, but the template still made me read and skip six full subsections. Type should gate which section even renders, not just which one to fill.
