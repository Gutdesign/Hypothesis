# Correcting 29 Construction-Tech Registry Profiles

## Executive Summary

- **Ownership Changes**: Blackstone completed Civica's acquisition in **May 2024**, OpenSpace acquired Disperse in **October 2025**, and Safety Plus acquired GoContractor in **July 2024** -> describe those products under their current owners. [7][13][9]
- **Different Business Models**: Q-Bot finances and develops robotics for retrofit, while PlanetVerify markets general identity and document verification -> do not classify either as an ordinary construction-project software supplier. [11][19]
- **Incorrect Funding Absence**: Asite received a **2024 secured credit facility**; this does not establish an equity round, but contradicts an unqualified claim of no external finance. [29]
- **Later Q-Bot Financing**: EMV Capital announced **£350,000 equity funding** and conversion of **£865,000 loans** in June 2025 -> the claimed September 2023 transaction is not the latest identified financing. [11]
- **Brand Versus Parent**: Byggfakta Group renamed itself Hubexo in **October 2024**; that does not make an estimate for the entire group NBS revenue. [6]
- **Misleading Product Labels**: Converge's investor identifies concrete management and decarbonisation, and Fifth Dimension describes decision intelligence for real assets -> narrow their categories accordingly. [30][1]
- **Unsupported Precision**: The accessible company and investor materials generally do not substantiate the registry's staff counts, cumulative fundraising totals or estimated sales -> retain those fields as unconfirmed rather than copying aggregator numbers.

**Reading the tables:** 'Not established' means the reviewed permitted material did not verify the registry claim, not that the underlying amount is zero. An undated live page is marked 'Undated'; an investment in a parent is not a funding round of its product brand. 'Marketed' means an accessible official product page, not independent proof of a recent sale.
