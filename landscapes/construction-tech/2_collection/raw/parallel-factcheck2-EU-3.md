# Primary-source recheck of 22 construction-technology brands

## Executive Summary
- **Registry Identity**: Several brand names differ from incorporated names, notably Boxcore and FYLD -> use the legal name in contracts [1][2].
- **Financial Caution**: None of the supplied aggregator revenue estimates was verified here as filed turnover -> do not use them as financial statements.
- **Office Caution**: A public contact address or city-level headquarters is not necessarily the registered office -> keep those fields separate.
- **Group Boundary**: Eleco and Sage group-level results must not be labelled as revenue of an individual product or brand.
- **Date Precision**: Only the incorporation dates explicitly visible in the primary results below are given as exact dates [3][4].
- **Evidence Gap**: This is a partial second check, not a completed audit of filed accounts -> the unknown cells require a filing-level review.

**Reading the tables:** "Registry value" reproduces the supplied, unverified input where applicable; "verified value" contains only facts supported by the primary results obtained. "Not established" means no suitable primary-source fact was obtained, not that a figure does not exist. Source date means the date this source was checked (2026-10-07), not the filing date. No funding claims are assessed because funding is not among the five requested table fields.

## Synthesis
The strongest identification here comes from a registry record or a company legal notice naming an incorporated entity. A brand name, an office on a website, and an aggregator estimate answer different questions: none substitutes for a registered address, financial-year average employment or filed turnover. The main outstanding work is to read each entity's latest filed accounts and establish its precise brand-to-entity relationship before using any missing figure.
