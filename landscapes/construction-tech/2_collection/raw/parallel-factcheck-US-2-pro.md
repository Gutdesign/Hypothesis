# Construction-Tech Registry Audit: Fix the Identity and Funding Errors

## Executive Summary

- **Wrong Headquarters**: Helonic's Y Combinator record places its two-person team in **New York City**, not San Francisco; Permitify's record places it in **Salt Lake City**, not San Francisco. Correct both locations, noting that a team location is not necessarily a registered headquarters. [24][25]
- **Wrong Founding Years**: Keyway says it was founded in **2021**, rather than 2020; Permitify's Y Combinator record says **2025**, rather than 2024. Update those entries. [26][25]
- **Misread Funding**: OpenSpace's **$102M** was its initial Series D financing, followed by **$9M** of additional capital; the company describes **$111M as the Series D total**, not necessarily its lifetime fundraising total. Replace the registry's lifetime-total claim with the narrower disclosed fact. [27][17]
- **Different Financing Instruments**: Keyway disclosed **$15M seed**, **$25M Series A**, and **over $100M in debt financing**. VTS announced a **$150M debt facility in March 2022** *before* its **over-$125M Series E in September 2022**. Keep equity rounds, debt facilities, and lifetime totals separate. [28][29][30]
- **Ownership Is Not A Funding Round**: Kiewit launched InEight as its wholly owned subsidiary in **2014**; Ethos Capital acquired Newforma in **April 2023**; Five Arrows announced a majority investment in NetVendor in **November 2025**. Classify these as ownership transactions, not venture rounds. [9][31][22]
- **Products Have Shifted**: Keyway now markets **AI workflows for real-estate investors**, beyond its original transaction-platform description. Parspec markets **quoting and project management for distributors and manufacturers' representatives**, rather than generic materials procurement. Update product categories to reflect the vendors' current descriptions. [11][32]
- **Hardware Is Material**: SubVysion's stated approach uses **autonomous rovers** to map buried utilities, and Xpanner sells construction-equipment automation. Neither should be described as a conventional software-only vendor. [33][34]
- **Aggregator Revenue Is Not Verified Revenue**: The stated revenue or ARR estimates for HqO, PermitFlow, Togal.AI, VTS, and Yardi were not substantiated by an allowed company disclosure in the materials reviewed. Keep those figures out of verified revenue fields. [14][19][10][30][35]

**Reading the tables:** "Unconfirmed (no primary source found)" means the particular registry assertion was not established by the permitted material reviewed; it does **not** establish that the assertion is false. An undated live website is labeled "undated." Y Combinator's own company records are used for information it reports firsthand about its portfolio companies, not third-party scraped profiles. "Still offered" reflects an active company product or sales page, not an independently verified purchase.

## Synthesis: Which Registry Errors Change the Company Description?

| Company or group | Mechanism of error | Correct description or action |
|---|---|---|
| **Helonic; Permitify** | Scraped geographic/founding details diverge from YC's firsthand company records | Helonic: New York City-based team and **Articulate AI, Inc.** legal entity. Permitify: Salt Lake City-based team, **2025** founding; distinguish both team location and registered HQ. [24][20][25] |
| **InEight; Newforma; NetVendor; Oracle C&E** | Subsidiary, acquisition, PE majority stake or business unit treated like an independent VC-backed startup | Kiewit subsidiary since **2014**; Ethos-acquired in **2023**; Five Arrows majority-backed in **2025**; Oracle business unit, respectively. [9][31][22][4] |
| **Keyway; Parspec; VTS; HqO** | Older or narrow product labels remain after broader positioning | Describe current **CRE AI workflows**, **distributor quoting/project management**, **asset-lifecycle software**, and **real-estate experience software**, respectively. [11][32][30][38] |
| **SubVysion; Xpanner** | "Construction tech" mistaken for software-only SaaS | Describe **hardware/rover-enabled utility mapping** and **construction-equipment automation**, with software components. [33][34] |
| **OpenSpace; Keyway; VTS; Togal.AI** | Funding-round size, debt facility, lifetime total and SAFE valuation cap conflated | Label **$111M Series D**, distinguish Keyway debt from equity, put VTS debt **before** Series E, and call Togal's **$50M a SAFE valuation cap**. [17][28][30][10] |

These are the entries most likely to mislead a reader about **what entity they are looking at, who controls it, what it sells, or what its financing figures mean**. None of the inspected primary materials established that any of the 24 had shut down; that is not proof that every product has completed sales, and undated live product pages should not be mistaken for audited operating statements.
