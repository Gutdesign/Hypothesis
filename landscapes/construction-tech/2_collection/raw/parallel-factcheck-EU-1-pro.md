# Construction Technology Registry: 31 Company Checks and Corrections

## Executive Summary

- **Builders Are Not Software Vendors**: 011h says it designs and constructs buildings, while Aridditive designs, manufactures and installs printed-concrete components. Both use digital technology, but neither should be described simply as a software vendor. [1][2] -> Classify by what the customer buys.
- **Products Are Not Independent Companies**: Cegid Sigrid is marketed as a Cegid product, and Ed Controls is presented as a Dutchview product. [3][4] -> Keep the product, operating entity and parent in separate registry fields.
- **Ownership Dates Need Precision**: Hilti announced its 4PS acquisition in July 2023 and reported regulatory approval on October 5, 2023; Planon described its July 30, 2024 transaction as an *agreement* to increase Schneider Electric's stake to 80%. [5][6] -> Do not turn an agreement into a verified closing.
- **Old Owners Can Mislead**: Kraan's sale to Nedvest closed on August 8, 2019, but ECI currently markets Kraan software; Visma subsequently acquired Roxit. [7][8][9] -> Record transaction history separately from the current product brand.
- **Funding Categories Differ**: MX3D itself reported a EUR7 million Series A in May 2025; SAALG itself reported a EUR3.65 million financing round in July 2023. Neither announcement verifies an aggregator's lifetime total. [10][11] -> Record round amounts without manufacturing cumulative totals.
- **Weak Estimates Stay Unverified**: The supplied employee counts, revenue estimates and many funding totals lack substantiation in the permitted sources reviewed. -> Do not silently promote aggregator estimates to facts.

**Reading the tables.** "Not verified" means no qualifying source found in this review, *not* that the assertion is false. "n.d." means the cited webpage does not establish a publication date. A live product page supports "currently offered," not proof of a recent sale. N/A registry fields are omitted unless a qualifying source established a value. The ownership row concerns the named company or product, rather than treating a parent's fundraising as its funding round.

## Synthesis: Registry Changes That Alter the Business Description

| Company or grouping | Registry risk | More defensible description |
|---|---|---|
| **011h, Aectual, Aridditive, MX3D** | Classifying everyone with a digital process as a SaaS vendor | Respectively a building-delivery business, printed-products business, printed-concrete project manufacturer and industrial metal-printing technology business. [1][16][2][10] |
| **Cegid Menfis GO, Cegid Sigrid, Ed Controls** | Treating a product label as an independently financed company | Verify the publisher separately; Sigrid is Cegid-branded, Ed Controls is a Dutchview product, and the specific Menfis GO-Cegid identification remains unverified. [3][4] |
| **4PS, Centric, Kraan, Planon, Pro4all, RIB, Roxit, RatedPower** | Recording acquisitions or stake transactions as "funding rounds," or freezing an old owner as current | Store owner, announcement/closing status and transaction date in separate fields. Planon's cited 80% event was an agreement, while Kraan's Nedvest sale closed in August rather than late 2019. [5][22][7][6][33][39][9][37] |
| **SAALG, Spotr, Struck, Vellum** | Missing a first-party round, asserting an unverified lifetime dollar total or retaining stale product positioning | Add SAALG's announced EUR3.65M round; preserve Spotr's and Struck's announced EUR amounts; describe Vellum's current GIS/planning-data platform. [11][41][45][46] |

The consequential distinction is **what is being sold** versus **what legal entity owns it**. A current product page can establish an offering, and an acquisition release can establish a transaction, but neither supplies an unreported company revenue, cumulative funding figure or present-day employee count.
