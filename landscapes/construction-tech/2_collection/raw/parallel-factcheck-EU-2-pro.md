# Correcting a 31-Company Construction-Technology Registry

## Executive Summary
- **Entity Classification**: RIB Candy is a product of RIB, whose takeover by Schneider Electric closed in **July 2020** [1]; classify the product under its parent, not as a venture-backed company.
- **Business Model Drift**: Kodehyve currently advertises e-signature, KYC/AML and operations infrastructure [2]; update the old developer-CRM category.
- **Founding-Date Errors**: Revizto says **2012** [3], while Zutec says **1998** [4]; replace registry dates **2008** and **1999**, respectively.
- **Investor Attribution**: Sablono says **Bachmaier Invest** led its **EUR5.3M** 2021 Series A [5]; correct the lead investor.
- **Funding Versus Other Capital**: CASAFARI reports a **$20M** extended Series A [6], while its separate **$120M** property mandate is not a corporate equity raise; keep mandate capital out of funds raised.
- **Unsupported Precision**: Several headcount, ARR and total-raised entries originate only from disallowed aggregators; mark these unconfirmed rather than promoting estimates to reported figures.
- **Category Boundary**: Smart Bricks describes property-investment analysis and advisory [7]; it should not be described as construction-project software.
