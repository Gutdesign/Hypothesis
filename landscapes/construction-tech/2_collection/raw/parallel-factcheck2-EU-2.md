# Construction-Tech Registry Check: Verified Identities, Missing Accounts

## Executive Summary

- **Legal Names Matter**: Bild AI identifies itself as Bild AI Inc., while Cosuno's legal notice names Cosuno Ventures GmbH. Use the legal entity, not just the product brand, in counterparty records. [1][2]
- **Registered Office Is Not Headquarters**: CMiC publishes a Toronto office and Revizto publishes a Lausanne headquarters, but neither statement establishes a registered-office address. Keep those fields separate. [3][4]
- **SkyCiv Needs a Name Check**: SkyCiv's own company page calls its Australian company E-Street Developers PTY LTD and gives ABN 73 605 703 071. The Australian Business Register result located for that ABN should be checked directly before treating the website's name as the *current* registered name. [5][6]
- **Nomoko Has a Material Status Flag**: The Swiss federal register search identifies Nomoko AG *in Liquidation*. That designation matters more than an unverified employee range. [7]
- **Employee Counts Need a Definition**: Buildots says it has "350+" talented people, and Cosuno describes "100+" employees; neither is a financial-year average from filed accounts. Do not substitute either for the requested accounting measure. [8][9]
- **Revenue Estimates Are Not Turnover**: No latest filed turnover or filed average employee figure was established for any of the 22 entities from the primary material located. Leave aggregator estimates out of verified financial fields.

**Reading the tables:** "Registry value" reproduces the supplied, unverified entry, not a finding from a government register. An office or headquarters advertised on a website is **not** called a registered office below unless a primary source identifies it as such. "Unconfirmed" means that the requested fact was not established by the primary sources located; it does not assert that no filing exists. Source date is "undated" when the cited page gives no usable publication or filing date. Funding entries supplied in the question are outside the five requested output fields and have not been validated.

## Synthesis

The strongest **identity** evidence in this check comes from company legal notices: CoCrafter, Optimuse, PlanRadar and Sablono publish register numbers, while Cosuno, Endra, Kodehyve and Revizto publish legal names without a fully verified registration-number-and-date combination in the material reviewed. [11][17][19][20][2][12][15][4] The strongest **status** evidence is different: Switzerland's official Zefix result names **Nomoko AG in Liquidation**. [7] The principal **location** risk is mistaking an operating address for a registered office; SkyCiv presents an additional legal-name/current-register discrepancy requiring resolution against its Australian ABN record. [3][4][5][6]

For the **financial** comparison, the defensible result is narrower than the supplied dataset: no latest filed turnover or financial-year average workforce was actually verified for these 22 entities. Company marketing counts, ARR estimates, business addresses and founding years must remain separate from filed accounting measures, registered-office addresses and incorporation dates.
