# Primary-Source Audit of 22 Construction-Technology Brands

## Executive Summary
- **Legal Identity Matters**: RatedPower identifies ENVERUS RATEDPOWER S.L.U. with tax ID B87769709, while Amrax identifies Synthetic Dimension GmbH with Firmenbuch FN 532171 f -> Use the legal names, not the brands, in entity records [1][2].
- **Product Is Not Company**: Cegid identifies Cegid SAS as the website owner; that does not establish the Spanish Menfis GO operator -> Leave the product-level entity open [3].
- **Group Is Not Subsidiary**: Centric's certificate names Centric Netherlands B.V. for relevant departments -> Do not assign whole-group staffing to VTH [4].
- **Address Types Differ**: RatedPower calls its Madrid address a main office, and ECI calls its Leusden address a visiting address -> Do not relabel either a registry-confirmed registered office [1][5].
- **Founding Is Not Incorporation**: MX3D says it was established in 2014, but gives no corporate registration date there -> Leave incorporation unconfirmed [6].
- **Transaction Timing Needs Correction**: The reported Kraan sale closed on 8 August 2019 rather than at year-end -> Correct the transaction note without treating the date as an incorporation fact [7].
- **Accounts Need Filing Evidence**: No company-specific filed turnover or average-employee figure was obtained in the evidence reviewed -> Keep all such figures unconfirmed instead of adopting the supplied external estimates.

The tables distinguish company-reported location from a *registered office*, business founding from *incorporation*, and a brand from its legally registered operator. "Unconfirmed" means the requested exact field was not established by an eligible primary document; it does not mean the input was disproved.

## Synthesis
The useful corrections concern **entity resolution**, not estimates: RatedPower and Metaroom name different legal operators, while Cegid Menfis and Ed Controls are products whose operators must not be inferred from group statistics [1][25][3][14]. A company-listed location or foundation year does not replace a register extract. The remaining records require entity-matched official extracts and filed accounts before any turnover or average-employment number can responsibly be populated.
