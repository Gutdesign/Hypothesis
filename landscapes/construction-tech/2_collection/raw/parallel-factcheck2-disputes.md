# Twelve construction-tech registry disputes: what primary records establish

## Executive Summary

- **Registered Office Is Not Headquarters**: UK Companies House records a London registered office for 1001 AI LIMITED [1] -> Do not replace the disputed headquarters field with that address without operational-HQ evidence.
- **Legal Name Is Not Incorporation State**: Helonic identifies Articulate AI, Inc. as its contracting legal entity [5], but the evidence gathered does not establish its state of incorporation -> Record the name and leave the state unresolved.
- **Debt Is Not Equity**: AshGrove describes its 2024 Asite financing as senior secured credit [9] -> Reject both the blanket "self-funded" claim and any inference that AshGrove acquired equity.
- **A Mandate Is Not A Raise**: CASAFARI separately described a $120M property mandate and a $15M Series A, subsequently extended to $20M [7][3] -> Do not add the mandate to company equity proceeds.
- **Round Labels Need Issuer Support**: Entrata called Blackstone's $200M transaction a minority investment on May 15, 2025 [10] -> Do not label it Series D on this evidence.
- **Corporate Birth Differs From Brand Origin**: Zutec says its business was founded in 1998 [12], but that does not establish an incorporation year for every Zutec entity -> Keep brand history separate from legal-entity dates.

A company's operating location, its registered office, its incorporation jurisdiction and its marketing "founded" year are different fields. The tables below resolve only what the permitted primary evidence supports. "Not established" means neither competing value is verified for the precise field asked.

Classify the transaction using the issuer's wording. "Minority investment" describes the stake; without a relevant Form D or issuer disclosure specifying a preferred series, it should not be promoted to a numbered round.

## Synthesis

These disputes divide into three different evidentiary problems. **Corporate identity** is comparatively strong where the company names its contracting entity (Helonic) or a register identifies a particular company (1001 AI LIMITED), but neither automatically supplies an operating HQ. **Corporate chronology** is weaker: a product's launch or a company's self-described founding year does not establish a certificate-of-incorporation date for ArchiLabs, Permitify, Keyway, Revizto or Zutec. **Financing classification** is stronger when the issuer or investor specifies the instrument: AshGrove's Asite credit is debt, Sablono names its Series A lead, CASAFARI distinguishes an equity round from a property mandate, and Entrata describes a minority investment rather than a numbered series [9][8][7][3][10].

The registry should therefore carry verified facts at their actual level of specificity: legal name without guessed state; registered office without guessed HQ; founding claim without invented incorporation date; and disclosed financing instrument without an aggregator's round label.
