# Construction-Tech Funding and Incorporation: 28-Company Primary-Source Audit

## Executive Summary

- **Funding Is Not Incorporation**: The supplied founding years do not establish incorporation dates -> keep those dates unverified unless a corporate filing or state record supplies them.
- **ALICE's Capital Mix Matters**: Its 2023 Series B extension was described in terms of access to **$47M in capital**, including financing beyond the originally announced **$30M** round -> do not enter $47M as equity raised. [1][2]
- **Built's Date Needs Correction**: Built announced its **$125M Series D on September 30, 2021**, not July 2022 -> correct the round date and do not append an unverified extension. [3]
- **Entrata's Transaction Is Not Labeled Series D**: Its own announcement calls the **May 15, 2025, $200M** Blackstone transaction a **minority investment** -> record that characterization rather than an unsupported round label. [4]
- **Dodge Has A Later Financing**: Dodge announced **$100M of new capital** in November 2024 -> distinguish its financing transaction from its private-equity ownership. [5]
- **Small-Company Totals Need Restraint**: Accelerator participation is not, by itself, company-specific proof of an amount raised -> leave the proposed $500K totals unverified where no qualifying disclosure was located.
- **EDGAR Can Name A Different Issuer**: A Togal.AI Form D identifies **Togal.AI, LLC** -> preserve the issuer's legal name rather than treating the brand as a verified corporate name. [6]

**Reading the tables:** "Not established" means the reviewed qualifying material does not establish that value; it does **not** mean that the company has no financing or that no filing exists. A founding year is not an incorporation date. No Form D amount-sold figure is compared with an announced round below unless the issuer and offering can be positively matched; amount sold to date is not automatically the completed round size. The URL and source-date cells are blank (`-`) where no qualifying support for that particular field was established.

## Synthesis

Across these companies, a release confirming an **investment**, one confirming a **round**, and a Form D reporting **securities sold to date** answer different questions. Entrata and NetVendor announced ownership investments; Dodge announced new capital alongside debt-maturity changes; Built and VTS announced named equity rounds. Treating all four types as comparable venture rounds would distort both dates and totals. [4][22][5][3][29]

The strongest corrections are **Built's 2021 date**, **Entrata's minority-investment label**, **Dodge's 2024 financing** and **Togal.AI's distinct Form D issuer name**. The main remaining gap is corporate identity: the supplied founding years and headquarters are not verified incorporation dates or jurisdictions. For registry use, retain a blank incorporation field rather than convert an operating-location or founding-year statement into a state filing. [3][4][5][6]
