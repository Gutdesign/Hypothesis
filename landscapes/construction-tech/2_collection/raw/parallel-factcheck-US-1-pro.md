# Construction-Tech Registry Audit: Correct the Identity-Changing Errors

## Executive Summary

- **Wrong Headquarters**: 1001 describes its business as GCC- and London-based, not San Francisco-based; Y Combinator places ArchiLabs in San Francisco, not Houston. Correct both location records. [1][2]
- **Ownership Is Not Funding**: Nemetschek's approximately **$100M** Bluebeam purchase and Hilti's Fieldwire purchase were acquisitions, not funding rounds received by those companies. Separate transaction history from capital raised. [3][4]
- **CoreLogic Changed Identity**: Stone Point Capital and Insight Partners completed their CoreLogic acquisition in **2021**; CoreLogic announced the **Cotality** name in **2025**. Record both the take-private transaction and rebrand. [5][6]
- **The Round Label Matters**: Entrata called its **May 2025 $200M** Blackstone transaction a *minority investment*, not a Series D. Do not add an unsupported round designation. [7]
- **An Accelerator Is Not A Disclosed Round**: ArchiLabs and FlowManual appear in Y Combinator's company listings, but those listings alone do not establish the registry's funding amounts or terms. Keep accelerator participation separate from a verified financing. [2][8]
- **Estimates Are Not Reported Revenue**: The supplied private-company ARR and revenue figures lack substantiation in the permitted company or transaction sources reviewed. Replace them with "not publicly verified," rather than treating an aggregator estimate as revenue.
- **Different Products Need Different Labels**: ALICE sells construction *optioneering*, ArchiLabs offers data-center design automation, and Fresco targets Division 8 takeoff and estimating. These are more precise than generic construction project management or estimating labels. [9][10][11]

**Reading the tables:** "Unconfirmed" means that the specified value was not established by an allowed source located in this review; it does **not** establish that the value is false. "Undated" means the cited page did not establish a publication date. A live product page is evidence that a product is currently advertised, not proof of a completed sale. Source URLs in the fifth column identify the evidence for that row; citation markers accompany material findings.
